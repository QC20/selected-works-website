Welcome to My Documents.
========================

This is a real file on the C: drive of this machine, not a picture of
one. It was written here the first time you opened the desktop.

Where it lives:
  My Computer > Hard Disk (C:) > My Documents > Notes

What you can do with it:
  - Open it in Notepad and edit it. File > Save writes it back.
  - Select it in My Documents and press Download to take a copy to
    your own computer.
  - Delete it. It will not come back.

Anything you write in Notepad and save with
File > Save As > My Documents\Notes (C:) turns up in the same folder.
Anything you draw in Paint and save to My Documents\Paintings turns up
next door.

None of it leaves your browser. The drive is kept in this browser's own
storage, which means it survives a reload and a visit tomorrow, but it
is not uploaded anywhere and I cannot see it.
